# Kazakhstan Ministry of Health Clinical Protocol: Liver Cirrhosis in Adults (2020, Protocol No. 135)

Source: diseases.medelement.com, RK MoH clinical protocol, condensed for MedAgent RAG index. Russian terms preserved alongside English for terminology matching against OCR'd Kazakhstani lab/discharge documents.

## Severity Grading — Child-Turcotte-Pugh (CTP)

Five parameters: ascites (асцит), encephalopathy (энцефалопатия), bilirubin (билирубин), albumin (альбумин), prothrombin time prolongation (МНО).

- Class A (5-6 points): compensated, median survival ~6.4 years.
- Class B (7-9 points): intermediate risk.
- Class C (10-15 points): median survival ~2 months without intervention.

MELD score (6-40) used for transplant prioritization since 2002; MELD-Na variant adopted since 2016.

## Disease Stages

- **Compensated (Class A)**: often asymptomatic for years, found incidentally, minimal portal hypertension.
- **Decompensated (Class B/C)**: ascites, variceal hemorrhage, encephalopathy, jaundice (желтуха) appear.
- Two sub-stages within compensation: minimal portal hypertension vs. clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH, hepatic venous pressure gradient >10 mmHg).

## Routine Monitoring Lab Markers

| Finding | Significance |
|---|---|
| Тромбоцитопения (thrombocytopenia) | hemolysis / splenic sequestration — expected in cirrhosis, not automatically alarming |
| Повышение АСТ/АЛТ | degree of hepatocyte injury (cytolysis) |
| Снижение альбумина | synthetic dysfunction |
| Повышение билирубина (общий/прямой) | cholestasis / impaired excretory function |
| Снижение ПТИ / повышение МНО | coagulopathy severity |
| Повышение креатинина | hepatorenal syndrome risk |
| Повышение мочевины | encephalopathy risk factor |

Etiology-specific markers relevant to PBC: АМА (anti-mitochondrial antibody), ANA, anti-LKM-1 — used to distinguish autoimmune/PBC etiology from viral or metabolic causes.

## Imaging

- **УЗИ (ultrasound)**: detects liver size change, increased parenchymal density with regenerative nodules, portal system dilation, splenomegaly, ascites. This is the imaging modality most patients will have a written report for.
- **FibroScan (непрямая эластометрия)**: METAVIR-based cutoff — F4 (cirrhosis) at >12.4 kPa, ~95.5% accuracy.
- **ЭГДС (upper endoscopy)**: grades esophageal varices F0-F3, assesses bleeding risk.

## Key Complications

### Hepatic Encephalopathy (Печеночная энцефалопатия) — West-Haven Grading

| Stage | Features |
|---|---|
| Minimal | Only detectable on psychometric testing, no clinical signs |
| I | Mild disorientation, sleep pattern changes, concentration lapses |
| II | Lethargy, time disorientation, personality changes, asterixis |
| III | Somnolence, spatial disorientation, confusion |
| IV | Coma, unresponsive |

Types: A (acute liver failure), B (portosystemic shunt), C (cirrhosis — most common, relevant here).

### Ascites (Асцит) — IAC Grading

- Grade 1: detectable only by ultrasound.
- Grade 2: moderate, visible symmetric distention.
- Grade 3: massive, tense abdomen.
- Refractory: unresponsive to sodium restriction + diuretics, or recurs quickly after large-volume paracentesis.

Treatment: Grade 1 — dietary salt restriction (80-120 mmol/day). Grade 2 — spironolactone 100-400 mg/day +/- furosemide 40-160 mg/day, target weight loss 0.5-1 kg/day. Grade 3 — large-volume paracentesis with albumin replacement.

### Hepatorenal Syndrome (Гепаторенальный синдром)

Type I: rapid deterioration, creatinine doubles to >220 umol/L within 2 weeks — an urgent/critical finding.
Type II: slower course with resistant ascites.

## PBC-specific note within this protocol

Ursodeoxycholic acid (урсодезоксихолевая кислота) 13-15 mg/kg daily is listed as first-line therapy for PBC (ПБХ); obeticholic acid as second-line if response is inadequate.

## Monitoring Intervals

- Viral cirrhosis: labs every 3 months, ultrasound/AFP every 6 months, EGD every 1-2 years (compensated, no varices) or 6-12 months (varices present / decompensated).
- Non-viral cirrhosis (including PBC): labs every 6 months, imaging/surveillance intervals adjusted to complication severity.

## Transplant Referral Triggers (for context only — never suggested by the app as a conclusion)

CTP Class B/C with progressive decompensation; MELD >15; refractory complications (encephalopathy, ascites, hepatorenal syndrome); HCC within Milan criteria.
